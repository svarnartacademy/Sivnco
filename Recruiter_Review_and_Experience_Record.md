## Verdict

I would shortlist you for a senior brand/packaging individual-contributor role or a hands-on design lead role at a consumer brand. I would consider you for a hands-on Art Director interview where the work is brand systems, packaging and visual storytelling. I would need more evidence before hiring you to manage a substantial design team, own a broad agency creative department or lead a mature product-design organization.

Your strongest proposition: you connect concept and illustration to a coherent commercial pack, then carry it into imagery, campaigns and production. Your production knowledge makes the creative work deliverable. It should support your creative identity, not replace it.

## What to avoid or deprioritize

- Creative Director/Head of Design roles requiring a large team, hiring/appraisal ownership and department budgets unless you can demonstrate those responsibilities.
- Senior UX/Product Designer or engineering roles whose core requirements are not supported by your current case studies. Keep digital experimentation as a strength rather than overstating specialization.
- Structural packaging engineering roles centered on material science, CAD testing and industrial validation; graphic packaging/prepress experience is not the same qualification.
- Pure performance-marketing roles requiring attribution, media buying and acquisition ownership.
- Routine social-media resizing or artwork-only roles when they offer no concept ownership or progression. They may be viable income choices, but can reinforce the execution-only positioning you want to move beyond.
- Blanket rejection of all Art Director roles. Titles vary; inspect responsibilities and required evidence.

## Evidence and interview preparation

Keep three short interview stories ready: a creative decision (DEB), a commercial/production decision (supplier/process choice), and collaboration under constraints (master pouch or corrected artwork release). For each, explain the situation, your decision, collaborators, result and what you would improve.

Do not send raw WhatsApp logs, internal financial ledgers, personal health details, CTC/ESOP letters or unredacted vendor contacts. Redacted proof can be available when appropriate. The supplied dossier's claim to be “verified” does not automatically validate every causal statement in it.

The refined PDF retains supplied commercial figures with careful wording; before submitting, ensure you can substantiate the quoted rates and order scope and are permitted to disclose them. If not, replace those two bullets with: “Compared supplier quotations and selected suitable print processes for trial and commercial volumes, balancing unit cost, materials and production requirements.”
